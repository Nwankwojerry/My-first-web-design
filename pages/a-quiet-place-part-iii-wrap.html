A Quiet Place Part III Wraps Filming as John Krasinski and Noah Jupe Look Ahead

Production on the next A Quiet Place movie has reached the finish line, with John Krasinski completing the trilogy's next chapter and Noah Jupe among the returning cast.

Production on A Quiet Place Part III has wrapped, marking a major step forward for the long-awaited continuation of John Krasinski's horror series. The film is now moving into the work that follows principal photography ahead of its scheduled theatrical release in 2027.

The trilogy returns to Krasinski's hands

Krasinski is back as the writer and director of the third mainline film, continuing the story after the original A Quiet Place and A Quiet Place Part II. The project is also positioned as the closing chapter of the trilogy he has been developing.

The returning cast includes Emily Blunt, Millicent Simmonds and Noah Jupe, with Cillian Murphy also back from Part II. Jack O'Connell, Jason Clarke and Katy O'Brian are among the newer additions to the franchise.

Noah Jupe remains part of the story

Jupe returns as Marcus Abbott, the role he played in the first two films. The latest wrap news gives fans their clearest production milestone yet for a movie that has taken several years to reach the screen.

The story details remain closely guarded, so there is still no confirmed plot breakdown to go with the production update. For now, the key news is that filming is complete and the film is on course for its planned theatrical arrival.

When will A Quiet Place Part III arrive?

Paramount has scheduled A Quiet Place Part III for release in theaters on July 30, 2027. With filming finished, attention will now turn to post-production and the eventual marketing campaign as the film moves closer to audiences.
